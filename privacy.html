Privacy notice.

How information submitted through this website is handled. This notice should be reviewed by a qualified professional and adapted to the final data-processing arrangements before launch.

01
Notice

What is collected

Only information you choose to submit: name, organisation, email address, phone number, enquiry category, budget indication and the content of your message. Newsletter subscription collects an email address and your explicit consent.

Why it is collected

To respond to your enquiry, to prepare a proposal where relevant, and — only where you have consented — to send the MindShift briefing. Data is not sold, rented or shared with third parties for marketing.

Spam protection

The enquiry forms use a honeypot field and require server-side validation once an endpoint is connected. No data is stored in browser local storage, and no secrets are held in front-end code.

Retention

Enquiry correspondence is retained only as long as necessary to manage the professional relationship and any legal obligations, then deleted or anonymised.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information at any time by writing to the address published in the website footer. You may withdraw newsletter consent in one click from any briefing email.

Third-party services

Fonts are served by Google Fonts and placeholder imagery by Pexels. Once form delivery is connected, the chosen email or form provider will process data on behalf of this site under their own terms.

Status: this notice is a working draft. Replace with the final version once the data-processing provider, storage location and retention period are confirmed.
