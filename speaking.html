03
Philosophy
Speaking philosophy

Substance first. Always.

A talk is not entertainment with content attached. It is a structured argument that leaves an audience able to do something differently on Monday morning.

  • Context before content. Every session is built after understanding the audience, the occasion and the outcome the organisers need.
  • Frameworks over slogans. Portable thinking people can reuse, not phrases they forget in the corridor.
  • Honesty over applause. Sessions say the difficult thing clearly, respectfully and with evidence.
  • A question to leave with. Each talk closes with something the audience must answer for themselves.
