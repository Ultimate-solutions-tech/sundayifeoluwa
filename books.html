Excerpt
Reading sample

From The Thinking Business

Working draft. The passage below is demonstration text written to show the reading experience. It will be replaced by the final manuscript.

Every business has one constraint. Not five — one. Everything else is a symptom of it, a distraction from it, or a consequence of it. The discipline of growth is the discipline of finding that constraint before you decide what to do about it.

Most teams skip this step, because diagnosis is slow and action feels productive. So they add staff to a broken process, run advertising into an unclear position, and hold more meetings around a decision nobody has actually made. Effort rises. Results do not.

Strategy is not the art of doing more. It is the art of being right about what to do first.

The alternative is unglamorous. Sit with the numbers until they tell you something uncomfortable. Interview the people closest to the work. Name the constraint out loud, in a sentence everyone can repeat. Then build the smallest set of systems that will move it — and review weekly until the evidence says you were wrong.

Building people. Strengthening businesses. Creating lasting value.
