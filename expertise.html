Business Growth

For owners and executives who need the business to grow deliberately rather than accidentally — with a diagnosis they can trust and a plan their team can run.

A.01

Growth Diagnosis

The problem

Growth has stalled and the reason is assumed rather than known — so effort is spent in the wrong place.

Why it matters

A wrong diagnosis makes a good strategy useless. Every shilling of effort is allocated before the constraint is confirmed.

The engagement

Structured review of performance data, market position, capacity, pipeline and constraints; interviews with the leadership team; a written diagnosis agreed by stakeholders.

Deliverables

Diagnosis report · constraint map · prioritised opportunity list · executive readout session.

Typical structure: 2–3 week diagnostic sprint Enquire
A.02

Business Strategy & Market Positioning

The problem

The business is competing on price because it has not decided what it is actually best at.

Why it matters

Undifferentiated positioning attracts the most price-sensitive customers and the thinnest margins.

The engagement

Market and competitor mapping, segment selection, value proposition design, offer architecture and a strategic plan with measures.

Deliverables

Positioning statement · segment priorities · offer architecture · 12-month strategic map.

Typical structure: 4–6 week strategy engagement Enquire
A.03

Revenue Strategy

The problem

Revenue targets exist but the route to them is not designed — pricing, pipeline and capacity are unlinked.

Why it matters

Targets without a mechanism become annual disappointment rather than a plan.

The engagement

Pricing review, revenue model design, capacity and conversion assumptions, forecasting rhythm and dashboard design.

Deliverables

Revenue model · pricing recommendations · forecast cadence · measurement dashboard spec.

Typical structure: 3–5 week engagement Enquire

Group C

Leadership Development

For organisations that need leaders at every level, not only at the top.

C.01 · Leadership training

Programmes that change behaviour, not attendance

Problem: leaders are promoted for technical performance and left to manage people without preparation. Engagement: needs assessment, cohort-based workshops, live practice, manager involvement. Deliverables: curriculum · participant workbooks · post-programme application plan.

C.02 · Team development

Teams that function under pressure

Problem: capable individuals who do not operate as a team. Engagement: team diagnostic, role and responsibility clarity, working-agreement design, facilitated sessions. Deliverables: team charter · role matrix · conflict and decision protocols.

C.03 · Strategic decision-making

Better judgement at leadership level

Problem: decisions made reactively, revisited repeatedly, or bottlenecked at the top. Engagement: decision frameworks, delegation design, meeting and review architecture. Deliverables: decision-rights map · framework library · review cadence.

C.04 · Organisational performance

Aligning people to the strategy

Problem: strategy at the top, disconnected activity below. Engagement: goal cascade design, measures, accountability rhythm, leadership scorecards. Deliverables: aligned objective tree · KPI framework · quarterly operating review.
