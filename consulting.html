01
Context
The pattern

Most businesses are not short of effort. They are short of structure.

Inconsistent sales, weak customer acquisition, unclear positioning, unstructured growth and leadership bottlenecks are rarely separate problems. They are usually one problem viewed from five angles: the business has not decided how it grows, and no system enforces the decision.

Consulting here begins by naming the actual constraint — not the loudest symptom — then building the smallest set of systems that will move it.

What an engagement involves.

01 — Business Growth Consulting

Find the constraint. Build the route around it.

A structured diagnosis of where the business actually stands, followed by a strategic direction and operating plan the team can run without me in the room.

Deliverables: growth diagnosis report · constraint map · prioritised opportunity list · strategic plan with measures · quarterly operating rhythm.

Growth has stalled and the reason is assumed rather than known.

Why it matters

Effort applied to the wrong constraint is the most expensive kind of work.

Structure

Diagnostic sprint → strategy build → implementation support. Typical horizon: 6–10 weeks plus review.

Turn selling from instinct into a repeatable process.

Pipeline design, qualification standards, stage definitions, proposal and pricing logic, and the coaching cadence that makes the process hold under pressure.

Why it matters

Without stages, there is no forecast, no coaching and no diagnosis of where deals die.

Process
How we work together

Four steps. No surprises.

Every engagement follows the same sequence, so you know what is happening, what it costs and what it should produce before work begins.

Step 01

Discovery call

A 30–45 minute conversation about the business, the constraint and whether I am the right person to help. No charge, no obligation.

Step 02

Written proposal

Scope, approach, deliverables, timeline, investment and success measures — agreed in writing before anything starts.

Step 03

Engagement

Structured work with scheduled checkpoints, working sessions with your team and a clear owner for each deliverable.

Step 04

Handover & review

Systems documented, internal owners confirmed, and a review against the measures agreed at the start.

Frequently asked
Do you work with businesses outside Nigeria?
Yes. Engagements are delivered remotely or on site by arrangement, and proposals state the delivery format in advance.
How are fees structured?
Fixed-scope projects for diagnostics and strategy builds; monthly retainers for implementation and coaching. Fees are quoted in writing after discovery — never improvised.
Do you guarantee results?
No honest consultant can. What is guaranteed is the quality of the diagnosis, the rigour of the work, and transparency about what is and is not moving against the agreed measures.
Can you work alongside our existing agency or consultants?
Yes. Most engagements involve existing teams and suppliers. Roles and handoffs are defined in the proposal to avoid duplicated work.
What do you need from us?
Access to the relevant data, one accountable sponsor inside the business, and honesty about what has already been tried.
